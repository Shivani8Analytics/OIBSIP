## 📌 Executive Summary
This project executes an advanced **Customer Segmentation Model** for an e-commerce platform using the **RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary)** framework combined with **Unsupervised Machine Learning (K-Means Clustering)**. Analyzing **391,148 transactional line-items** across **4,333 unique customers**, this analysis partitions the customer base into distinct behavioral cohorts to drive personalized marketing strategies, maximize Customer Lifetime Value (CLV), and reduce customer churn.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives
1. **Data Ingestion & Hygiene:** Filter non-customer transactions, eliminate duplicate records, cast datatypes, and verify non-negative line items.
2. **RFM Feature Engineering:** Synthesize customer-level transactional behaviors into three core quantitative metrics:
   * **Recency ($R$):** Days elapsed since the last recorded order date.
   * **Frequency ($F$):** Total count of unique orders placed by a customer.
   * **Monetary ($M$):** Total revenue generated by a customer.
3. **Data Standardization:** Apply `StandardScaler` ($\mu=0, \sigma=1$) to normalize feature variance prior to distance-based metric calculations.
4. **Optimal Cluster Selection:** Evaluate Within-Cluster Sum of Squares (WCSS/Inertia) across $K \in [1, 10]$ using the **Elbow Method**.
5. **Algorithmic Segmentation:** Fit `K-Means` clustering ($K=3$) and construct granular behavioral profiles.
6. **Data Visualization:** Map segment decision boundaries using scatter plots (outlier-controlled) and distribution bar charts.

## 🛠️ Data Pipeline & Preprocessing Audit
* **Initial Raw Volume:** `527,756` transaction records.
* **Duplicates Removed:** Dropped `10,062` duplicate line items $\rightarrow$ Updated shape: `522,535` rows.
* **Missing Value Handling:** Dropped `131,387` records missing a `CustomerID` (mandatory for RFM aggregation).
* **Final Analysis Set:** `391,148` clean, verified transactional records.
* **Data Integrity Checks:** Confirmed $0$ entries with negative/zero `Quantity` or `UnitPrice`.

## 📈 Exploratory Statistics & Baseline Metrics
* **Average Purchase Value ($APV$ per Order):** `$461.50`
* **Average Purchase Frequency ($APF$):** `4.25` orders/customer
* **Customer Lifetime Value ($CLV$):** `$1,959.74`
* **High Standard Deviation Variance:** Monetary standard deviation (`$8,461.15`) severely outpaces the mean (`$1,959.74`), proving extreme variance in spending behavior and confirming the need for non-linear segmentation models.

## 🔬 K-Means Clustering & Profiling Results

## 💡 Strategic Business Insights & Actionable Recommendations

### 1. Cluster 0: Regular / Core Buyers (`3,220` Customers)
* **Behavior:** Highly engaged with recent interactions (41 days) and steady recurring purchases.
* **Strategy:** Drive cross-selling and increase order values.
* **Actionable Plan:** Enroll in an automated loyalty program; send personalized product recommendations based on previous category purchases.

### 2. Cluster 1: At-Risk / Inactive Customers (`1,089` Customers)
* **Behavior:** High churn velocity with an average dormancy period exceeding 8 months (246 days) and low total spend.
* **Strategy:** Re-engagement and win-back campaigns.
* **Actionable Plan:** Deploy triggered "We Miss You" email sequences offering time-sensitive 15% discount vouchers to reactivate account activity.

### 3. Cluster 2: VIP Champions & Wholesalers (`24` Customers)
* **Behavior:** Ultra-high monetary value (averaging **~$82.4K** per customer) purchasing every 6 days.
* **Strategy:** High-touch white-glove retention and relationship management.
* **Actionable Plan:** Provide dedicated account managers, exclusive early access to product launches, and tier-based bulk wholesale pricing structures.
